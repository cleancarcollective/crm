# Packages — what we sell, ranked by what people actually buy

Last 6 months of completed/confirmed bookings, both shops combined.
Use this to anchor recommendations: most people land on the top 3,
so unless the customer has a specific reason (heavy interior soiling
= Premium Interior; pre-sale prep = Premium Detail), default to one
of those.

---

## Tier 1 — the bread and butter

### 1. Combination (Interior + Exterior) — 84 bookings, avg $350

The default for someone who just wants the whole car sorted. Outside
hand-wash, inside vacuum + plastics, windows, the works. Three to four
hours on our end. **Most customers actually want this** even when they
ask for "a detail" without specifying.

**Pitch line**: "We'll do inside and out — full hand wash, vacuum,
interior detailed, plus a paint sealant. About $370 for a sedan,
takes us about 3-4 hours."

### 2. Deluxe Detail — 64 bookings, avg $378

Same scope as the combination above but named/sold as a single
package. Includes:
- Complete exterior hand wash + dry
- Interior vacuum + plastics detailed
- Wheel + tyre cleaning
- Door jambs + windows
- 3-month paint + plastic sealant

**Pitch line**: "Deluxe is our most popular regular detail — exterior
hand wash, interior vacuum, plastics + windows, plus a 3-month
sealant. Around $370 for a sedan."

### 3. Premium Detail — 37 bookings, avg $635

The "deep clean / fresh start" pass. Includes everything in the
Deluxe PLUS:
- Clay bar treatment
- Spot paint correction (minor scratches)
- Wax application + protection
- Engine bay cleaning
- Interior shampoo treatment

**Pitch line**: "Premium is our full restoration package — everything
in the Deluxe plus clay bar, wax, engine bay, and interior shampoo.
Best if it's been over six months since the car's last proper detail,
or you're prepping it for sale. About $625 for a sedan."

## Tier 2 — when they want one side only

### 4. Exterior Only options — 29 bookings (varies by package)

For customers who only want the outside done. Two sub-options:
- **Deluxe Exterior Hand Wash** — $116 avg. Basic + sealant.
- **Premium Exterior** — $365 avg. Includes clay bar + wax.

**Pitch line for upsell**: "If you're getting the outside done, our
Deluxe Detail does the inside too for an extra $250-ish — most
customers find the value's there."

### 5. Interior Only options — 23 bookings (varies by package)

- **Deluxe Interior** — $256 avg. Vacuum, plastics, windows.
- **Premium Interior** — $404 avg. Shampoo carpets + seats, deep
  clean, stain extraction.

**When to recommend Premium Interior over Deluxe**:
- Pets / kids / construction / farm work
- Stains, odours, mould
- Hasn't had an interior done in 12+ months

## Tier 3 — speciality / coating work (usually not phone sales)

These are higher-margin but typically need a quote based on the car's
condition. Don't try to close on the phone — book them a free quote /
consultation visit.

### 6. Ceramic Coating — 6 bookings combined, big variance

Three tiers:
- **Bronze** (1 year warranty)
- **Silver** (2 year warranty)
- **Gold** (5 year warranty) — avg $1,053 for the standalone gold

Customers asking about coatings should ALWAYS book a consultation
first — the price depends on paint condition. **Do not quote a fixed
price over the phone.**

**Pitch line**: "Coating's a great option, but we always want to see
the car first to give you an honest quote — the price varies a lot
based on paint condition. Want me to book you in for a 15-min
consultation? It's free."

### 7. Paint Correction — 5 bookings

- **1-Step** — $550 (single Sedan reference)
- **2-Step** — varies, typically $800-1500+

Same rule as ceramics: book a quote visit. The 2-step + Gold ceramic
combo we did in May was $1,825 — that's the sweet-spot premium combo
for someone serious.

### 8. Headlight Restoration — 2 bookings, $120 each

Add-on / standalone. Easy upsell on any other booking.

## Things people ask for that we usually don't push

- **Ozone treatment** — only as part of a Premium Interior + ozone
  combo for smoke/odour cases. Don't lead with this.
- **Mold treatment** — same. Quote-based, always book a visit.
- **Free consultation** — useful for ceramic / paint correction
  prospects; sometimes books on the spot at the appointment.
