Welcome. Three docs in this folder, in the order to read them:

1. **`01-packages-by-popularity.md`** — what we sell, ranked by what
   people actually buy. Memorise the top 3.
2. **`02-pricing.md`** — exact price tables. Quote in NZD, GST

3. **`03-objections.md`** — the cold-lead playbook. The opener, the
   common objections, and how to close.

## How the workflow goes

1. Log into the CRM at https://crm.cleancarcollective.co.nz with the
   credentials Max gave you.
2. You'll land on the **Sales queue** — a list of cold leads
   (people who enquired 1-6 months ago and never booked) scoped to
   your shop.
3. Each row has the customer's name, number, vehicle, what they
   originally asked about, and how long since their enquiry.
4. **Sorted by least-recently-touched** — so you naturally work
   through without double-calling the same person.
5. Click a row → goes to the customer's profile. You can see their
   full enquiry history.
6. Call them (tap the phone number to dial on mobile).
7. After the call, leave a note in the lead's profile (call outcome
   + anything useful).
8. Update the lead's **status**:
   - **Contacted** — spoke to them, no booking yet
   - **Callback** — they asked us to call back
   - **Won** — booked them in
   - **Lost** — they declined / went elsewhere / fake number
9. If they book — hit the **"Book this customer →"** button at the
   top of their profile. The booking form opens with their info
   pre-filled. Confirm the service + date + time + price.

## Discount authority

- **Up to 5% off** any service — you can offer to close on the call.
- **First-time-customer voucher** ($20 off services over $300) —
  use sparingly.
- **More than 5%** — ping Max before promising. Don't commit.

## What you can and can't do in the CRM

You CAN:
- See cold leads for your shop
- Read customer profiles + history
- Add notes to leads
- Update lead status
- Create bookings via the "Book this customer" button

You CAN'T:
- Edit customer names / emails / phone numbers (notes only)
- See the main calendar, other staff, settings, analytics
- See leads from the other shop (Wellington vs Christchurch)
- Edit existing bookings — if a customer wants to reschedule
  something already booked, hand off to Max

## When something needs Max

Ping Max for:
- Refunds or complaints
- Discounts > 5%
- Ceramic coating / paint correction quotes
- Reschedule requests on existing bookings
- "Can I talk to the owner?"
- Anything weird

## Tracking

Max can see your stats at `/sales/stats` — calls made, wins, revenue
booked, conversion rate. Not to micromanage — to know what's
working so we can do more of it.

## One rule

Be the kind of person you'd want to talk to if it were your number
being called. Friendly, brief, no pressure, give them the out. The
best closers don't push — they ask good questions and listen.

Good luck.
