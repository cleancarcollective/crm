# Pricing — the only number you need to memorise

Christchurch and Wellington run the same prices. Quote in NZD, GST
inclusive. Always exact dollars — never "around" if the customer's
already given you their vehicle.

## How to find the right tier

**Vehicle types** (ask: "What car is it? Year, make, model?"):
- **Coupe / Hatchback** — most hatchbacks, small coupes
- **Sedan / Wagon** — most sedans, station wagons
- **Small / Medium SUV** — RAV4, CX-5, X-Trail, Outlander
- **Large SUV / Ute** — Highlander, Prado, Hilux, Ranger, Patrol

If unsure, default UP a tier (better to over-quote and adjust down
than under-quote and explain a higher charge on the day).

## Mobile (we come to them) vs in-store

Same prices either way. Mobile uses our own water + power. Customer
needs to confirm there's space + power if mobile.

**When to suggest mobile**: customer has young kids, no spare day,
office park parking. Premium Detail mobile = we usually need to keep
the car overnight regardless, so in-store is more efficient for that
package.

## Discount authority — what you can offer

- **5% off** any service if the customer's been quoted higher
  elsewhere and is on the fence. Mention it as "I can do 5% if you
  book in today."
- **First-time-customer voucher** ($20 off any service over $300) —
  use sparingly, max one per shift.
- **Anything above 5%** — ping Max or Ben for approval. Don't promise
  it without checking.

## Recurring discounts (use as a closing hook)

If the customer's a regular driver who'll need it again, offer to
lock in a recurring slot:

| Cadence | Discount | Deluxe Sedan/Wagon |
|---|---|---|
| Every 2 months | **15% off** every visit | $314.50 |
| Every 3 months | 10% off | $333 |
| Every 4 months | 5% off | $351.50 |

Frame it: "If you want me to lock you in every 2 months, I can knock
15% off — that's $314.50 instead of $370. You can cancel or pause
any time. Worth thinking about?"

The CRM books the first one, and the system auto-generates the next
ones + sends reminders.

## What you CANNOT quote over the phone

- Ceramic coatings (Bronze / Silver / Gold)
- Paint correction (1-step / 2-step)
- Anything described as "really dirty / mould / smell / heavy stains"

For these: book a **free 15-minute consultation** at the shop. We
quote on the day after seeing the car. Better to under-promise on
the phone than over-promise and have to walk it back.
